What Is a Relationship History and Timeline?

A relationship history and timeline presents the development of a relationship in a chronological and easy-to-understand format. It organizes important milestones in the order they happened — when the relationship began, significant meetings, periods of communication, trips and time spent together, family introductions, important events, and engagement or marriage milestones where applicable.

The purpose is to present the relationship clearly and consistently rather than simply listing isolated dates. By arranging milestones in a logical sequence and linking each one to relevant context, the document helps a reviewer understand the relationship as a whole — how it began, how it grew, and where it stands today.

At NEPSOP, we help applicants prepare relationship history and timeline documents that are professionally structured and aligned with the supporting information provided. We never fabricate or exaggerate relationship history: each document is built from the genuine details the applicant shares and presented accurately.

A relationship history and timeline is a chronological record of relationship milestones. An SOP or statement, on the other hand, is a narrative document that explains circumstances, purpose, and supporting context in the applicant’s own story.

The two documents often complement each other. Many applicants prepare a relationship history alongside a dependent visa statement, and we make sure both remain consistent with the same dates and details.

A well-structured timeline presents events in a clear sequence, keeps the language simple, and stays consistent with the supporting documents. Every milestone is easy to find, and references to photographs, travel records, correspondence, or certificates help connect each entry to evidence.

The goal is a document that reads naturally and can be absorbed quickly, without lengthy paragraphs or unexplained gaps.

Yes. Every relationship history and timeline we prepare is based on the information the applicant provides — real dates, real experiences, and real supporting documents. Accuracy and honesty are essential because the document is meant to reflect genuine circumstances.

If information is missing or unclear, we work with the applicant to understand the details before the document is finalized.

A relationship history and timeline may be requested or helpful in partner and spouse visa applications, dependent visa applications, and other situations where the development of a relationship needs to be explained clearly.

Requirements vary depending on the visa category, country, and application circumstances. Applicants should always follow the requirements of the relevant immigration authority and confirm whether such a document is expected for their application.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about relationship history and timeline preparation

What is a relationship history and timeline?

A relationship history and timeline is a document that presents the development of a relationship in chronological order. It organizes milestones such as when the relationship began, significant meetings, communication, visits, family introductions, and engagement or marriage milestones so the relationship can be understood clearly and consistently.

Which applications might need a relationship history and timeline?

A relationship history and timeline may be requested or helpful in partner or spouse visa applications, dependent visa applications, relationship-based immigration applications, and other situations where a chronological account of the relationship is relevant. Requirements vary depending on the visa category, country, and application circumstances.

Is the timeline based on my actual relationship history?

Yes. Every relationship history and timeline we prepare is built from the information the applicant provides. We never fabricate, exaggerate, or misrepresent relationship history or supporting evidence. Applicants share their genuine milestones, dates, and documents, and we present them clearly and consistently.

What information do you need from me?

We typically need the key dates and details of your relationship, such as when it began, first meetings, periods of communication, visits and travel, family introductions, and engagement or marriage milestones where applicable. Supporting materials such as photographs, travel records, correspondence, and certificates help us keep the document consistent.

How is this different from a dependent visa SOP or statement?

A relationship history and timeline is a chronological record of relationship milestones, while an SOP or statement is a narrative document that explains circumstances and purpose. The two often complement each other. We can prepare relationship history documents alongside dependent visa statements and keep both consistent.

How long does it take to prepare a relationship history and timeline?

Typically, a relationship history and timeline takes 3-5 business days from the time we receive your details. Revisions are available so the final document accurately reflects the information you provide.
